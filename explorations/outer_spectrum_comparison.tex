\subsection{Locked \(\delta=.09\) projection table}

The comparison holds the full-split EBCH inner ledger fixed and swaps only the local outer model.  The script invocation
is
\[
\texttt{python scripts/compare\_outer\_modes\_fullsplit.py --delta 0.09}.
\]
It writes
\[
\begin{gathered}

\texttt{scripts/outer\_mode\_comparison\_delta009.md}.
\end{gathered}
\]

The modeled BCH-like local spectrum used in the projection is even-weight:
\[
A_w=0\quad(0<w<d_0\text{ or }w\text{ odd}),\qquad
A_w\approx 2^{k_0-(n_0-1)}\binom{n_0}{w}\quad(w\ge d_0,\ w\text{ even}),
\]
with optional sensitivity inflation of \(+10,+20,+40\) bits on \(d_0\le w\le d_0+32\).  At \(N=2^{21}\),
\(\delta=.09\), and \(d=188743\), the baseline projection rows are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c|c}

\]
All three rows are dominated by the first-active near-window row \(r=1\), \(1\le\mathrm{gap}\le4000\).  The rough outer
work proxy \(B n_0\log_2 n_0\) is \(18874368\) for the \(512\)-bit rows and \(16777216\) for the \(256\)-bit row.

The sensitivity check is important.  The \([256,128,38]\) model remains near the RM margin with no inflation and
has \(27.154731\) bits of margin under \(+10\) bits, but the projected \(501\le h\le2000\) row becomes dominant and
positive under \(+20\) bits.  The \([512,256,\ge62]\) model is more robust in this diagnostic: its margins under
\(+10,+20,+40\) bits are \(65.625088\), \(55.894300\), and \(35.894577\) bits, respectively.  These rows stay heuristic
until an exact BCH spectrum or a rigorous low-weight envelope replaces the modeled spectrum.

\paragraph{August 2026 construction and obstruction audit.}
The local-code object itself is now exact.  The parity extension of the primitive narrow-sense
\(B(511,61)\) code has checked parameters \([512,259,\ge62]\).  Intersecting it with
\(x_0+x_1=x_0+x_2=x_0+x_3=0\) raises the check rank from \(253\) to \(256\), retains the all-one word, and gives a
deterministic complement-symmetric \([512,256,\ge62]\) subcode.  The complete check matrices and a canonical
generator basis are regenerated and authenticated by
\(\texttt{scripts/build\_bch512\_256\_candidate.py}\); no true-distance or spectrum assumption enters this step.

The spectrum remains the obstruction.  The public Okayama index has no linked central-dimension length-256 table,
and its published 2021 extension covers only \(k\le71\) and \(k\ge187\).  More sharply, the 2019 ZSSMP study at
\(\url{https://doi.org/10.11591/ijece.v9i2.pp1232-1239}\) reports \(BCH(511,259)\) as one of the two length-511
cases for which even the true minimum distance was not determined.  Thus an exact full weight distribution for this
specific parent would be substantially beyond the public data located in this audit.

Parameter-only finite bounds do not replace it.  The parent dual zero set contains \(0,1,\ldots,14\), and the parity
extension therefore has dual distance at least \(16\).  This gives an exact strength-15 orthogonal-array Christoffel
bound; distance \(62\) also gives a constant-weight packing bound.  Against the corrected even-weight projection
(including \(+40\) bits on its sensitivity window \(62\le w\le94\)), the best of these two rigorous bounds is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
w & \log_2 A_w^{\rm target} & \log_2 A_w^{\rm generic} & \text{remaining gap}\\
\hline
62  & 53.426584  & 110.264015 & 56.837431\\
94  & 132.734477 & 192.655529 & 59.921052\\
118 & 139.180519 & 221.066516 & 81.885997
\end{array}
\]
The exact arithmetic and a hashed JSON ledger are generated by
\(\texttt{scripts/audit\_bch512\_spectrum\_obstruction.py}\).  This does not show that the desired spectrum envelope
is false.  It shows that distance, complement symmetry, and dual strength alone leave more than fifty bits unresolved:
the next successful step must be BCH-specific cancellation, a specialized orbit/coset enumeration, or a different
local code with a published spectrum.  Also, the \(h>2000\) row has not been recomputed for the BCH model, so the
low-weight projection is not by itself a theorem upgrade.
