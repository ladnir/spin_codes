\section{Disclosure of generative AI use}
\label{app:ai-disclosure}

The core ideas underlying this work originated with the authors and had
been developed over several years before the AI-assisted phase of the
project. The subsequent work used generative AI extensively, including
ChatGPT 5.6 and 6.0. This was a human-directed research collaboration,
not an independently conceived research project delegated to an AI model.

All manuscript text was generated through iterative prompting under close
human supervision. This includes the main text, proofs, appendices,
captions, and this disclosure. Editorial changes were also implemented
through prompts: the authors directed changes to the organization,
exposition, terminology, and interpretation of results. The use of AI
therefore extended beyond language correction or copyediting.

Developing and completing the proofs, selecting concrete instantiations,
and producing the implementations involved close collaboration between
the authors and AI models. The models contributed proposed arguments,
calculations, code, experimental workflows, and revisions. The authors
set research objectives and constraints, questioned proposed claims,
identified issues requiring further investigation, and directed additional
analysis and experiments. Numerous iterations were required to refine
the arguments, parameters, implementations, and presentation; the results
were not obtained by accepting a single generated answer.

This account distinguishes the human origin of the core ideas from the
substantial role of AI in their technical development and presentation.
The authors retain responsibility for the claims, attribution, and
correctness of the submitted work, including all AI-generated material.
