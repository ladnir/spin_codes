\section{Disclosure of Generative AI Use}
\label{sec:ai-disclosure}

The core ideas originated with the authors several years before this
AI-assisted work. The proofs, concrete instantiations, experiments,
and implementations were developed through close human--AI collaboration,
with numerous iterations directed by the authors. The manuscript was drafted
with AI assistance over many iterations, with substantial revision and
direct editing by the authors throughout.
We used GPT-5.6 for most of this work and GPT-6.0 only during the final phase.

The process involved thousands of prompts; a complete transcript is not
included because of its volume. The authors retain responsibility for all
claims, attribution, and correctness, including AI-generated material.
