\section{Finite RM/EBCH certificate}
\label{sec:rm-ebch-finite-certificate}

The current finite theorem uses a direct sum of \(4096\) copies of \(RM(4,9)\),
a binary \([512,256,32]\) code, as the outer code.  Hence
\[
k=4096\cdot256=2^{20},\qquad N=4096\cdot512=2^{21}.
\]
The inner is the full-split construction of
Section~\ref{sec:fullsplit-local-code-inner} with the exact EBCH
\([128,64,22]\) local spectrum.  The target relative distance is
\(\delta=.09\), so
\[
d=\lfloor .09N\rfloor=188743.
\]
The authoritative verifier is
\[
\begin{gathered}
\texttt{python scripts/verify\_fullsplit\_finite\_ledger.py}\\
\texttt{--write-manifest-json scripts/fullsplit\_finite\_ledger\_manifest.json}.
\end{gathered}
\]
The verifier is part of the finite certificate.  It checks the exact local
spectra used in the outer and inner models, the finite row-family bounds in the
first-moment ledger, and the coverage bookkeeping that every nonzero outer
weight and every inner placement regime used by the certificate is included.

The finite certificate input data are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{component} & \text{certified object}\\
\hline
\text{outer spectrum} & RM(4,9)\ [512,256,32]\ \text{exact local spectrum}\\
\text{outer direct sum} & 4096\ \text{blocks},\ N=2^{21}\\
\text{inner split law} & EBCH\ [128,64,22]\ \text{exact local spectrum},\ b=64\\
\text{target} & \delta=.09,\ d=188743\\
\text{manifest} & \texttt{scripts/fullsplit\_finite\_ledger\_manifest.json}.
\end{array}
\]

\paragraph{Checked ledger summary.}
The manifest records the exact RM support floor, the exact-prefix ledger, and
the post-prefix row cover.  The paper-facing values are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{checked item} & \text{manifest value}\\
\hline
\text{minimum positive outer weight} & 32\\
\text{all first-active placements for }32\le h\le500 & \log_2\mu\le -37.278528\\
\text{checked row families for }h\ge501 & \log_2\mu\le -182.259739\\
\text{total checked first moment} & \log_2\mu\le -37.278528 .
\end{array}
\]
The dominant row is \(h=32\), first-active regime \(r=1\), gap bucket
\(1\le\mathrm{gap}\le4000\).  The detailed row family table is recorded in the
manifest and supporting certificate notes.

\begin{theorem}[Checked finite RM/full-split first-moment certificate]
\label{thm:fullsplit-rm-finite-certificate-009}
Consider the finite checkpoint \(N=2^{21}\), \(b=64\), and \(\delta=.09\), so
that
\[
d=\lfloor .09N\rfloor=188743 .
\]
Use the direct-sum \(RM(4,9)\) outer and the full-split EBCH inner above.  Let
\(Z_d\) be the number of nonzero concatenated codewords whose final weight is
at most \(d\), with expectation taken over the random interleaver and the
random choices of the inner construction.  The checked finite ledger gives
\[
\mathbb E Z_d\le 2^{-37.278528}.
\]
Consequently
\[
\Pr[d_{\min}\le d]\le 2^{-37.278528}.
\]
In particular, with positive probability a sampled code has
\(d_{\min}\ge188744\), and hence relative distance
\[
\frac{d_{\min}}{N}\ge \frac{188744}{2^{21}}>.09 .
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:block-outer-first-moment-interface} reduces the failure
probability to the first moment \(\sum_hA_h^{\rm out}p_h^{\rm in}(.09)\).  The
manifest-backed ledger summary above removes all positive weights below \(32\),
covers all first-active placements for \(32\le h\le500\), and covers the
checked \(h\ge501\) row families.  Its log-sum gives
\(\log_2\mu_{\rm checked}(.09)\le -37.278528\), which is the displayed
expectation bound.  Markov's inequality applied to the nonnegative integer
random variable \(Z_d\) gives the probability statement.
\end{proof}
