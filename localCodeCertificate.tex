\subsection{Finite RM/EBCH certificate}
\label{sec:rm-ebch-finite-certificate}

The finite instantiation uses the direct sum of \(4096\) copies of the binary
\(RM(4,9)\) code with parameters \([512,256,32]\).  Thus the outer code has
\[
 k=4096\cdot256=2^{20},\qquad N=4096\cdot512=2^{21}.
\]
The inner is the full-split construction of
Section~\ref{sec:fullsplit-local-code-inner} with \(b=64\) and the extended-BCH
\([128,64,22]\) local weight distribution.  We take
\[
 d=\lfloor .09N\rfloor=188743.
\]

\paragraph{Inputs and conventions.}
The certificate consumes the following committed integer weight tables:
\begin{quote}
\texttt{scripts/rm512\_256\_spectrum.csv}\\
\texttt{scripts/EBCH128\_64.wd}.
\end{quote}
The verifier checks their dimensions, total multiplicities, minimum positive
weights, complement symmetries, and SHA-256 fingerprints.  These checks
establish internal consistency and bind the certificate to the committed
tables; the verifier does not derive either table from a code definition, so
their mathematical provenance---in particular that of the EBCH table---remains
a declared input.

There are \(B=N/b=32768\) inner blocks, \(S_0=0\), and no terminal-state
constraint; \(S_B\) is discarded.  For a nonzero interleaved outer word, let
\(i=\min\{j:U_j\ne0\}\) be its first active input block and set
\[
 T:=B-i+1.
\]
The ledger partitions \(T<5949\) as the ultra-late regime and writes
\(T=5949+\mathrm{gap}-1\) in the remaining gap buckets.  Within a block, \(r\)
denotes input occupancy, \(q\) state occupancy, and \(e\) the number of
turnoff episodes used by the episode expansion.  These cases form an exhaustive
nonnegative decomposition of the slice-to-tail probability.

For the exact outer enumerator \(A_h^{\rm out}\) and the inner slice probability
\(p_h^{\rm in}(.09)\), define
\[
 \mu_{\le500}:=\sum_{h=1}^{500}A_h^{\rm out}p_h^{\rm in}(.09),\qquad
 \mu_{\ge501}:=\sum_{h=501}^{N}A_h^{\rm out}p_h^{\rm in}(.09).
\]
The exact RM support check also proves \(A_h^{\rm out}=0\) for
\(1\le h\le31\).

\paragraph{Arithmetic model.}
All spectrum coefficients, placement counts, split probabilities, and final
dyadic comparisons are integers or exact rational numbers.  For \(h\le500\),
the six gap sums are exact and the Chernoff envelope is rounded upward on a
1024-bit dyadic grid; the remaining episode tails and final aggregation are
rational logarithms are evaluated with correctly rounded decimal logarithms at
rational.  For \(h\ge501\), real logarithms occur only through outward
intervals: integer and rational logarithms are evaluated with correctly rounded decimal logarithms at
rational logarithms are evaluated with correctly rounded decimal logarithms at
130 guard digits, enlarged by \(10^{-110}\), and every subsequent operation is
rounded outward at 100 digits.  Binomial logarithms use exact small factorials
or Robbins bounds together with \(333/106<\pi<355/113\).  Every threshold
comparison uses the upper interval endpoint.  The cached post-prefix artifact
is authenticated by its payload hash and by the hashes of both spectrum inputs;
its full recomputation reproduces the stored payload.

The authoritative default check is
\[
\begin{gathered}
\texttt{python scripts/verify\_fullsplit\_finite\_ledger.py}\\
\texttt{--write-manifest-json scripts/fullsplit\_finite\_ledger\_manifest.json}.
\end{gathered}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Complete low-weight row certificate]
\label{lem:fullsplit-rm-ebch-low-rational}
For every first-active placement and episode case with \(1\le h\le500\),
\[
 \mu_{\le500}\le \frac{6793}{2^{50}}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The exact direct-sum RM coefficients leave 114 positive weights in this range.
The ultra-late regime is summed as an exact rational.  The remaining six gap
buckets retain \(e=0,\ldots,8\) in the three prefix buckets and
\(e=0,\ldots,16\) in the three early buckets; outward rational tails cover
\(e\ge9\) and \(e\ge17\), respectively.  Exact cross multiplication of their
sum against \(6793/2^{50}\) passes.  The cached exact gap sums and outward
dyadic inner bounds are SHA-256 authenticated, and their regeneration commands
are recorded in the manifest.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Complete post-prefix row certificate]
\label{lem:fullsplit-rm-ebch-high-rational}
For every first-active placement and episode case with \(501\le h\le N\),
\[
 \mu_{\ge501}\le 2^{-180}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The outward artifact covers the critical \(501\ldots2000\) rows, ultra-late
placement, prefix cap and fixed-\(T\) episode rows, prefix paired-\(T\) rows,
early endpoint and paired rows through \(N/2\), and the complement-high range
through \(N\).  Exact rational checks establish the global turnoff envelope
below \(2^{-62}\), all fixed-pole MGFs, adjacent-ratio peak locations, and 56
endpoint-in-\(T\) monotonicity reductions.  The resulting outward aggregate
upper endpoint is \(-183.800029543464\), which is below the stated dyadic
threshold.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational/outward finite RM/full-split certificate]
\label{thm:fullsplit-rm-finite-certificate-009}
For the ensemble above, let \(Z_d\) count nonzero outer codewords whose
concatenated output has weight at most \(d=188743\), including a zero output if
the inner map has a nonzero kernel element on the outer code.  Then
\[
\Pr\Bigl[
 \Ctwo\circ\Pi\big|_{C_{\rm out}}\text{ is not injective}
 \ \text{or}\ d_{\min}(\Csc)\le d
\Bigr]
\le \mathbb E Z_d
\le \frac{6793\cdot2^{130}+1}{2^{180}}
\le 2^{-37.27}.
\]
Consequently, with positive probability the sampled concatenated encoder is
injective on its \(2^{20}\)-dimensional outer code and its image is a binary
\[
 [2^{21},2^{20},d_{\min}\ge188744]
\]
code.  In particular \(188744/2^{21}>.09\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:block-outer-first-moment-interface} bounds the joint bad event by
\(\mu_{\le500}+\mu_{\ge501}\).  The two certificate lemmas give
\[
 \mathbb E Z_d
 \le \frac{6793}{2^{50}}+\frac1{2^{180}}
 =\frac{6793\cdot2^{130}+1}{2^{180}}.
\]
Writing \(K=6793\cdot2^{130}+1\), the verifier checks the exact integer
inequality \(K^{100}\le2^{14273}\), which is equivalent to the final
\(2^{-37.27}\) comparison.  Since this bound is below one, some realization has
\(Z_d=0\); that realization is injective on the outer code and has the claimed
dimension and minimum distance.
\end{proof}
