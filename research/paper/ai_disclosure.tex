\section{Disclosure of Generative AI Use}
\label{sec:ai-disclosure}

The core construction predates this AI-assisted work. The proofs, concrete
instantiations, experiments, and implementations were developed through close
human--AI collaboration over many author-directed iterations. The manuscript
was drafted with AI assistance and revised substantially by the authors.
We used GPT-5.6 for most of this work and GPT-6.0 only during the final phase.

AI tools also assisted numerical-certificate generation and verification.
A separate Lean formalization of an earlier IMT construction involved
Claude and GPT assistance. A new Lean development checks Scalable SPIN at its
native lengths, including distance, rate, and fixed-setup ordinary and
transposed circuit bounds. Another Lean development, written with Claude
assistance, checks the Turbo SPIN distance bounds at $K=2^{18}$ and
$K=2^{20}$.

The process involved thousands of prompts; a complete transcript is not
included because of its volume. The authors retain responsibility for all
claims, attribution, and correctness, including AI-generated material.
