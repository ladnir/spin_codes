# What is committed here

This directory publishes the mathematical arguments and research notes from
the BCH spectrum work. Start with [RANDOM_INNER_M22_CLOSURE.md](RANDOM_INNER_M22_CLOSURE.md)
for the final ideal-M22 theorem and its scope.

The local research workspace also contains computational witnesses, exact
rational solutions, verification programs, historical experiments, sampling
tapes, third-party tools, and source PDFs. Those files are deliberately not
part of this Git snapshot. Nothing was deleted from the local workspace.

References to `code/`, `generated/`, `data/`, `paper/`, and other local
artifact paths in these notes describe that retained workspace. The replay
commands require those omitted files and cannot run from this notes-only
checkout. This snapshot is not a self-contained computational certificate.

The final numerical result was checked in the retained workspace. Independent
reproduction requires a separately packaged, authenticated certificate set;
the Markdown statements alone do not replace its computational witnesses.
The large historical sampling tapes are not assumptions of the final
deterministic-spectrum M22 theorem.

Older notes preserve the reasoning behind unsuccessful and intermediate
approaches. Their status statements are historical; the final theorem note
and the newest entry in PROGRESS.md identify the current result.
