# Sources

## Primary spectrum paper

Toru Fujiwara and Takuya Kusaka,
"The Weight Distributions of the (256,k) Extended Binary Primitive BCH Codes
with k <= 71 and k >= 187,"
IEICE Transactions on Fundamentals, E104-A(9), 1321--1328, 2021.
DOI: 10.1587/transfun.2020EAP1119.

A copy supplied by the user is in `paper/e104-a_9_1321.pdf`.

The tables used here are:
- Table 7: complete `EBCH(256,71)` spectrum.
- Table 8: low weights of `EBCH(256,187)`.
- Table 10: complete spectrum of `EBCH(256,187)^perp`.
