\section{Accumulator warmup notes}
\label{sec:exploration-accumulator-warmup-notes}

The accumulator warmup is kept in the main paper only through its exact enumerator and low-output contraction interface.
The optional sharpenings below are preserved here rather than compiled into the current theorem spine.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Derive a symmetric upper-tail analogue for \(\Pr[\wt(\Acc_n(U))\ge (1-\delta)n]\) and combine the two tails in a
single bound.
\item Optimize the \((4e\delta)^k\) constant by tightening the ratio
\(\binom{n}{w-k}/\binom{n}{w}\) and using a sharper bound on
\(\sum_{h\le \delta n}\binom{h-1}{k-1}\).
\item If needed for a particular region of the outer spectrum, extend the contraction bound to all \(w\le \mu n\) for any
fixed \(\mu<1\) by tracking
\[
\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}\frac{w-i}{n-w+i+1}
\]
as \((w/(n-w))^k\) instead of \((2w/n)^k\).
\end{enumerate}
