# Proof status

## Fixed constituent

The fixed map `A` is generated by the constant function, all seven linear
functions, and eight selected quadratic functions on \(\mathbb F_2^7\).
Thus its image is a subcode of \(\mathrm{RM}(2,7)\). Complete enumeration of
all \(2^{16}\) image words gives minimum distance 48.

The constant and linear functions make the 128 coordinate forms nonzero and
distinct. Every coordinate form has constant coordinate one. Therefore no
three coordinate forms sum to zero. The first three factorial moments of the
enumerated spectrum satisfy the required identities.

The construction defines `B` as the transpose of `A`. Degree counting proves
`BA=0`. The row space of `B` has the same spectrum as the image of `A`.
Applying the exact MacWilliams transform gives the complete kernel spectrum
of `B`. The kernel has minimum distance four and contains 64 words of weight
four.

## Regular theorem

Let the message length be \(2^{20}\), and let the encoded length be
\(N=2^{21}\). Set

\[
d:=\lfloor0.09N\rfloor=188743.
\]

Assume the modeled regular spectrum of the binary \([256,128]\) outer code.
Setup samples the outer coordinate permutations, region permutations, and
nonzero epoch scalars independently. For the fixed maps in this folder, the
expected number of nonzero regular messages with output weight at most
\(d\) is at most

\[
2^{-80.1685}.
\]

The one-active calculation contributes the limiting term. Its margin is
80.1685 bits. The aggregate margin for occupations 2 through 8192 is
125.5200 bits.

The calculation uses the exact image spectrum of `A` in the fixed-code shell
average. It uses the exact kernel spectrum of `B` for zero-state activation.
The PreAddMul state lemma applies with \(2^{16}-1\) nonzero states.

## Scope

The theorem is conditional on the modeled outer spectrum. The calculation
uses nearest binary64 arithmetic. It does not cover mixed occupations with
at least two all-one outer words and more than 64 total active blocks.

The state-size exploration does not prove that 16 bits are necessary. A
fixed 15-dimensional subcode of \(\mathrm{RM}(2,7)\) fails near occupation
2560 by 6807.05 exponent bits. A different 15-bit constituent could perform
better.
