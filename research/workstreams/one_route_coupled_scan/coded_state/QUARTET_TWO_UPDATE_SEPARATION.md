# A two-update availability-separation bound

2026-10-31. A 513-state transfer now retains every other canonical
availability-separation condition exactly. Small rational checks and an
independent source audit pass. One bounded floating diagnostic improves
the unrestricted availability count by about 208230 bits, but its combined
routing bound remains positive. This is a secondary checked alternative;
it does not close a new whole-code class or change the construction.

The profile and probability conventions are those of the
[run/occupancy certificate](QUARTET_RUN_OCCUPANCY_CERTIFICATE.md).
The separation condition below applies to canonical availability A, not
to the separate actual-state support profiles S.

## The condition retained inside a pair

For an availability history A_0 empty, A_1,...,A_T on n=512 coordinates,
write

    G_i = Gamma_i(A_(i-1)),       E_i = G_i \ A_i,
    L = sum_i |A_i|,
    D = sum_i |A_(i-1) \ A_i|,   G = sum_i |G_i|.

The partitions Gamma_i are independent and uniform. Every realizable
canonical history satisfies

    G_i intersect E_(i-1) = empty.                         (1)

Its footprint has F-f=G. Here E_0 is empty and the canonical terminal
condition is E_T empty. The bound in this note drops the terminal
condition, as well as half the conditions (1). There is no expansion of
A_T and no closure after update T.

Let P(u,k) be the probability that a fixed u-set meets k quartets in a
uniform partition. Conditional on this occupancy, the hit union outside
the u-set is a uniform (4k-u)-subset. For an excluded e-set disjoint from
the u-set, the weighted fresh-partition factor is therefore

    M_sep(u,e;z) = sum_k P(u,k) z^(4k)
                    * C(n-u-e,4k-u) / C(n-u,4k-u).       (2)

Terms with impossible indices are zero. The intermediate E in the
two-update transfer is generated by the first partition and tested by
the second. It is never chosen independently by a worst-case bound.

## The positive 513-state transfer

For positive markers s,t,z, let W=s^L t^D z^G. Define

    T_uv = s^v [y^v](t+y)^u(1+y)^(n-u),
    M_u = sum_k P(u,k) z^(4k),
    K = diag(M) T.

K is the unrestricted one-update transfer. To retain (1) at the second
update of a pair, put B=C T, where

    C_uv = s^v sum_k P(u,k) z^g
             * sum_j [y^j](t+y)^u(1+y)^(g-u)
                       C(n-g,v-j) M_sep(v,g-j;z),
    g = 4k.                                                (3)

The first hit union has g positions; j of them belong to the intermediate
availability mask. Thus its excess set has size g-j. Equation (2) tests
this set before the second new mask is summed by T.

There is a positive cubic construction of C. Let a be the number of
positions in the second hit union outside the intermediate availability
mask, and define

    J_ua = sum_k P(u,k) z^(4k) C(n-4k,a).

Choosing these a positions first and applying Vandermonde to (3) gives

    C_uv = s^v sum_a P(v,(v+a)/4) z^(v+a) J_ua
                    / C(n-v,a)
                    * [y^v](t+y)^u(1+y)^(n-a-u).           (4)

Only u+a<=n and v+a divisible by four contribute. The a extra positions
must avoid the first hit union, which explains C(n-4k,a). The remaining
intermediate mask avoids these a positions, which explains the last
coefficient in (4).

For N'=n-a, stream the coefficient arrays

    H_N'[0,v] = C(N',v),
    H_N'[u,v] = t H_(N'-1)[u-1,v]
                 + H_(N'-1)[u-1,v-1].                     (5)

Only two arrays are needed. Forming J is a positive matrix product;
(4)-(5) require O(n^3) operations and O(n^2) storage. The implementation
normalizes coefficient rows and keeps their masses in logarithmic
scales. Finite-range underflow raises an error rather than dropping a
positive term. Floating arithmetic remains diagnostic arithmetic.

For T=2m updates, let Z_even retain (1) at even updates and Z_odd retain it
at odd updates. Independence of disjoint partition pairs gives

    Z_even = e0 B^m 1,
    Z_odd  = e0 K B^(m-1) K 1.

Dropping constraints gives the weighted canonical bound

    sum_canonical A E[W] <= min(Z_even,Z_odd).              (6)

No union penalty is needed when selecting the smaller bound. Cauchy--
Schwarz also gives the geometric mean of the two parity moments, but
that is weaker here. Both moments use the same markers. A fixed (L,D,G)
profile count is bounded by (6) divided by s^L t^D z^G. The independently
derived routing and field factors can then be applied to that same
profile. Actual birth f, terminal E_T, and occupied-region constraints
have not been restored by (6).

At t=1 there is an additional identity. Write
b_v=C(n,v)s^v, mu_u=M_u, and a=C1. Then T=1 b^T, B=a b^T,
K=mu b^T, and a_0=b^T mu. Consequently both parity moments equal

    (b^T mu) (b^T a)^(m-1) (b^T 1).

The generic implementation does not require this special-case shortcut.

## Checks and the bounded diagnostic

[quartet_two_update_separation.py](quartet_two_update_separation.py)
is standalone. The root agent ran all checks and probes serially under
the existing 175-second, 2-GiB and 20-GiB-free-space guards.

The exact checks passed 106 C entries from independent labeled-mask and
quartet-partition enumeration at widths four and eight, with 131 census
terms. All 424 floating C,T,B,K entries agreed with the rational values.
Two width-four full mask/excess dynamic programs checked the parity
formulas, free endpoints, and domination of the full separation event.
An independent read-only audit verified (2)-(6), normalization, and the
shared matrix powers.

One full-width geometry diagnostic used

    log2(s) =  1.7817767338002406,
    log2(t) =  0,
    log2(z) = -1.6385879999612136,
    L = 1310720,   G = 1687040.

The reported log2 moment was 1027682.743857562. The resulting history
count exponent was 1456635.8427854765, compared with
1664865.6320033427 for the unrestricted occupancy moment: a gain of
208229.7892178662 bits. Runtime was 9.143 seconds.

For the associated availability diagnostic
(h,R,L,f,F)=(7168,917504,1310720,33280,1720320), the routing exponent was
about -799828, leaving a positive combined upper bound of about 656808.
This does not establish a low-weight codeword, a realizable profile, or
failure of the construction. The analogous numerical tuple in the
actual-state analysis has different statistics and must be assessed
under its own theorem.

Keep this helper as a secondary comparison. The next proof work should
cover the common remaining profile classes using the current actual-state
and adaptive-moment bounds, rather than extend this single parity probe
into another unrestricted parameter search.
