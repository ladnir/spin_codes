\section{Structured local-code construction}
\label{sec:structured-local-code}

The second main construction line is local-code independent.  The outer is
specified by a short local code and its spectrum, the inner is specified by a
short rate-half local code and its full-split transition law, and the global
distance statement is a finite first-moment certificate of the form
\[
\sum_h A_h^{\rm out}p_h^{\rm in}(\delta).
\]
Two finite checkpoints are proved below.  The frozen full-dimension theorem
uses an RM outer and an EBCH full-split inner.  A second theorem uses 128-coordinate
EBCH outer blocks together with one componentwise-XOR parity block; it is the
variant tailored to encoding vectors over \(\F_{2^{128}}\).  BCH-like codes
without certified spectra or low-weight envelopes remain upgrade projections.
