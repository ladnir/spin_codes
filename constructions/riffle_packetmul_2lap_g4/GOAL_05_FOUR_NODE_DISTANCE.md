# Goal 05: exact four-node observation distance

**Status:** complete with a bounded solver obstruction. The authenticated
interval remains \(36\le d(C_4)\le42\). See
`proof/GOAL_05_FOUR_NODE_DISTANCE_REPORT.md`.

## Objective

Determine the exact minimum distance of the four-node autonomous observation
code. Goal 04 proves a lower bound of 36 and records a codeword of weight 42.
Goal 05 must close or narrow the interval

\[
36\le d(C_4)\le42.
\]

## Observation code

Let \(U:\mathbb F_2^{64}\to\mathbb F_2^{64}\) be the autonomous output map

\[
U(o):=\operatorname{Acc}(P(o)).
\]

Define the binary linear code

\[
C_4:=\left\{
\bigl(o,U(o),U^2(o),U^3(o)\bigr):o\in\mathbb F_2^{64}
\right\}.
\]

The first 64 coordinates equal \(o\), so \(C_4\) has dimension 64 and length
256. The code is systematic and has no nonzero word generated by \(o=0\).

## Exact decision problem

The primary decision instance asks whether there exists \(o\ne0\) such that

\[
\operatorname{wt}\bigl(o,U(o),U^2(o),U^3(o)\bigr)\le41.
\]

An `UNSAT` result, together with the authenticated weight-42 word, proves
\(d(C_4)=42\). A satisfying assignment is an exact counterexample to that
claim and must be replayed through the recurrence.

## Work packages

1. Reconstruct \(C_4\) and authenticate the Goal 04 weight-42 word.
2. Encode all 256 output bits as linear functions of 64 information bits.
3. Impose a nonzero information word and total output weight at most 41.
4. Solve the primary instance with native XOR constraints.
5. Reconstruct the matrix independently and solve a pure-CNF encoding.
6. Replay every satisfying witness outside the solver model.
7. If the instance is unsatisfiable, propagate distance 42 into the prefix
   cutoff and the support-33 ledger.

## Completion criteria

The goal is complete when one of the following outcomes is authenticated.

- Both exact encodings return `UNSAT`, and the weight-42 word replays.
- One encoding finds a word of weight at most 41, and an independent recurrence
  replay confirms it.
- A solver cannot finish within the bounded session, and the report records
  the exact unresolved instance, solver state, and next proof mechanism.

## Probability statement

The observation-code result is deterministic. If it improves the cutoff, the
support-33 event with nonzero first-lap terminal state closes pointwise. The
proof must not assume that packet multipliers remain independent after
conditioning on a nonzero terminal state.

## Excluded work

This goal does not bound terminal-zero events at supports above 33. It does not
prove the full construction. It does not run a performance benchmark.

## Outcome

The native-XOR instance returned `UNKNOWN` after 60 seconds. An independent
pure-CNF reconstruction returned `UNKNOWN` after exactly 100,000 conflicts.
Both encodings replay the known word of weight 42. Neither produced a word of
weight at most 41 or an unsatisfiability proof. Thus Goal 05 does not determine
the exact distance.

Under the unresolved predicate, every one of the four blocks has weight from 9
through 14, and at least one block has weight 9 or 10. Exhausting those two
weights costs 179,013,799,328 anchors per information set, or 716,055,197,312
naive anchor-alignment visits. This scale explains why extending the Goal 04
enumeration directly is not the next bounded proof step.
